# VoidOS

**A "CustomOS" project built on Microsoft Windows 10.**

Debloated, optimized and privacy-oriented Windows, packaged for gaming
and productivity — plus clean, self-contained tooling to keep it that way.

## What VoidOS does

All optimizations are applied through a structured, reversible pipeline:

- **Debloating** — removal of Edge, OneDrive, Teams and unused AppX
  packages.
- **System tuning** — service configuration, registry tweaks, scheduler
  and power-plan settings.
- **Latency reduction** — background-process and timer optimizations
  for gaming.
- **Privacy** — telemetry and tracking reduction, selective cleanup,
  delivered through local, source-available tooling.
- **Branding** — consistent themes, cursors and wallpaper across
  installations.

---

## Tooling philosophy

- **Local by default** — the control panel never phones home; everything
  runs on your machine.
- **Transparent** — the debloat and cleanup logic is bundled and
  inspectable in the source-available repos.
- **Elevated where it matters** — the panel runs as administrator so
  registry, services and priority tweaks can do their job.
